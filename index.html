Set up in five steps

TrenchAstra is in beta, so for now you load it into your browser yourself. You only do this once.

  1. Download and unzip

    Press the download button above, then unzip the file. You get a folder called trenchastra. Keep it somewhere permanent, like Documents. Chrome reads the extension from that folder every time it starts.

  2. Open your extensions page

    In Chrome, Brave or Edge, paste this into the address bar. Browsers block links to it, so copy it here.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    Switch on Developer mode in the top-right corner of the extensions page.

  4. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped trenchastra folder, the one that contains manifest.json. TrenchAstra now appears in your list.

  5. Open a token page and reload it

    Go to a token page on GMGN, Axiom, Padre, Photon or BullX and reload the tab. The TrenchAstra panel appears on the right. Press Alt+P to hide or show it, and drag its header to move it.

    If a price or market cap looks wrong, open Settings in the panel and tap Re-detect. To link a number by hand, tap Pick Market Cap and click the number on the terminal.
Updating later: replace the files inside the same folder, then press the reload icon on the TrenchAstra card in chrome://extensions. Using the same folder keeps your history and settings.

What you get

Everything runs inside your browser, on top of the terminal you already trade on.

Trade on the real page

Open a token on your terminal and buy with fake money. The panel reads the live price and market cap from the page.

Take profit and stop loss

Set TP and SL on every position, or save defaults for all new buys. A TP can sell just part of the position, and a win or a stop plays its own sound.

Quick buttons you control

Edit the buy amounts and sell percentages in one tap, then save your settings without resetting your account.

PnL that adds up

Selling half a position keeps its total PnL steady. Fees and slippage default to 0.5% and you can change both.

PnL cards to share

Turn any open or closed trade into a clean PNG card with your entry and exit market cap, win or loss. Share it on X in one click.

Average buy line (beta)

Show a dotted line on the chart at your average buy, with your live PnL, plus lines for your TP and SL.

Stats that tell the truth

Win rate, profit factor, expectancy and streaks, with a CSV export of every trade.

Side panel

Keep your account open beside any tab with Chrome's side panel, and trade from there while you browse the terminal.

Private by design

No account and no wallet connection. Your trades live in your browser and never leave it.

Works on GMGN, Axiom, Padre, Photon and BullX. GMGN and Axiom are the most tested. Padre support is still improving.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no fees and no tracking.

Does it touch my wallet or real money?

No. TrenchAstra is a simulation. It never connects to a wallet, never signs anything and never sends a transaction.

Will my paper results match real trading?

Not exactly. Real fills, speed and liquidity differ, and a simulation cannot predict them. Raise the fee and slippage settings to be more cautious.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Your balance, trades and settings are stored locally in your browser. The extension makes no network requests of its own.

Why do I have to load it manually?

TrenchAstra is in beta and is shared as a file for now. Loading the folder takes about a minute and you only do it once.

Is it affiliated with GMGN, Axiom or any other terminal?

No. TrenchAstra is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any trading terminal.
